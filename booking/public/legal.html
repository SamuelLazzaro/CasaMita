Dati trattati dal servizio per la propria sicurezza

Per proteggere il servizio da accessi abusivi registriamo l'indirizzo IP e l'orario dei tentativi di accesso alla dashboard e degli invii dal modulo di prenotazione. I contatori per indirizzo IP vengono cancellati entro 24 ore; il registro degli accessi alla dashboard, con gli accessi falliti e il loro indirizzo IP, viene conservato per 12 mesi. Base giuridica: il legittimo interesse del gestore del servizio alla sicurezza del sistema (art. 6, par. 1, lett. f, GDPR). Questi dati non vengono usati per altre finalità e non vengono comunicati a terzi, salvo l'hosting su piattaforma Cloudflare.

Who runs the service

The booking service is run by [PROVIDER NAME], [ADDRESS], email [EMAIL], VAT number [VAT NUMBER], on behalf of the venues that use it. For every booking the data controller is the venue: its privacy notice is linked from the booking form and from the emails.

Data the service keeps for its own security

To protect the service from abuse we record the IP address and the time of the sign-in attempts to the dashboard and of the submissions from the booking form. The per-address counters are deleted within 24 hours; the dashboard sign-in log, with the failed attempts and their IP address, is kept for 12 months. Legal basis: the legitimate interest of the service operator in the security of the system (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These data are used for no other purpose and are not shared with third parties, apart from the hosting on the Cloudflare platform.

Cookies

The public pages use no cookies. The dashboard and the reserved areas use a technical session cookie, strictly necessary for signing in, which requires no consent. The chosen language is saved in the browser's localStorage.
